<h1 align="center">Hi there, I'm Zi-ang Cao ✨</h1>

![visitors](https://visitor-badge.laobi.icu/badge?page_id=Zi-ang-Cao.Zi-ang-Cao)

I'm a PhD student in computer science at the University of Texas at Austin. I work with my advisor, Prof. Yuke Zhu, in the [Robot Perception and Learning Lab](https://ut-austin-rpl.github.io/).

During my M.S. at Stanford, I worked with Jingyun Yang and Prof. Jeannette Bohg on equivariant imitation learning for mobile manipulation, and with Prof. Karen Liu in The Movement Lab. I earned my B.Eng. at the University of Pittsburgh in 2023.

## 🌱 Research Interests

I study how manipulation skills transfer from people to robots and between robots with different bodies. Two problems I work on are measuring progress during long tasks and controlling how a humanoid responds to contact as it moves.

## 🔬 Recent Research

- **Grana** tracks progress on multi-step manipulation tasks. If a part slips during insertion, the robot's progress score can drop while earlier assembly steps still count. Co-first author, November 2026. [Project](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/grana/) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24926) · [Code](https://github.com/NVlabs/Grana)
- **GR00T 2** learns from human video and robot demonstrations. Its Turbo variant uses SONIC to coordinate walking and manipulation on a humanoid. NVIDIA whitepaper, November 2026. [Project and demos](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/gr00t-2/) · [Whitepaper](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/gr00t-2/assets/gr00t2_whitepaper.pdf)
- **CHIP** lets a humanoid adjust the stiffness of its hands as it moves, so the same controller can wipe a board or hold a box steady while running. Co-first author, CoRL 2026. [Project](https://nvlabs.github.io/CHIP/) · [Paper](https://nvlabs.github.io/CHIP/static/pdf/CHIP_Adaptive_Compliance_CoRL2026.pdf) · [Code](https://github.com/Ericcsr/CHIP_release)
- **SONIC** uses human motion capture to train a single controller for humanoid teleoperation and autonomous manipulation. Core contributor. Published in ***Science Robotics***, August 2026. [Project](https://nvlabs.github.io/GEAR-SONIC/) · [Journal article](https://doi.org/10.1126/scirobotics.aed4592) · [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07820) · [Code](https://github.com/NVlabs/GR00T-WholeBodyControl)

[More publications on my website](https://zi-ang-cao.github.io/#publications)
